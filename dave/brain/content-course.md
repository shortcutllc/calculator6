### Step 1 — 4 traps to watch (getting started is the make-or-break step)
The course's 4 failure modes for getting started, with Dave's Shortcut-specific read:
1. **Fear of judgment** — the #1 blocker, never fully goes away. Will's to feel; antidote the
   course implies = volume/consistency makes any single post low-stakes.
2. **Overthinking** — planning content that goes stale before you post. *This is OUR trap.* We
   have a 3-version spine, a VOC reality check, and a month-old unresolved tension. Strategy is
   a head start, not a reason to keep polishing before publishing. Dave's job: push back if we
   treat "refine the framework" as a substitute for "post the thing."
3. **Overextending** — every platform at once → burnout, no traction. For a B2B wellness
   founder the single lane is almost certainly LinkedIn. Decide formally when course reaches
   platform; bet against splitting.
4. **Self-centered** — posting commercials for your business instead of giving value first.
   *We already beat this in the outreach lane* — Dave's hardest rule is "most touches ask for
   nothing" (give-first); brand voice bans perks theater. Extend the same instinct to content.
   Raw material we own: why participation is the only honest wellness metric, why the category's
   ROI stats are invented, 90%+ booked / 87% rebook / Wachtell 119 as evidence of delight by
   behaviour. Trust-building posts that never mention a deal.

Takeaway to carry: keep going, keep it simple, post before it's perfect.
