# Shortcut Go-To-Market Plan (one-pager)

## 1. Positioning — the hub everything hangs on
**Top line: "Wellness your people show up for."**
One outcome, two mechanisms (NOT three equal claims):
- **Outcome (lead here, prove it):** people actually show up. Near-total participation vs ~24% for typical wellness (Gallup). The part of the budget that doesn't sit unused.
- **Mechanism 1:** zero lift. One team runs it start to finish; you approve a date and do nothing.
- **Mechanism 2:** one vendor for your whole team, in office and remote.

**The one thing only we can say:** proven participation at scale, 90%+ of slots booked, 87% of clients rebook, 500+ companies, BCG and DraftKings at every US office. Behaviour, never invented ROI.
**Voice:** calm, human, no perks theater, never sell the tech.

## 2. The GTM web — one system, not five campaigns
Positioning is the hub. The levers aren't separate campaigns; each does one job around the same core:
- **Open new doors** the cold market won't: Conferences, CLE.
- **Unlock the budget** so price stops killing deals: Carrier wellness funds (cuts across broker AND client).
- **Deepen + expand** what's open: Retreats/offsites, then land-and-expand into recurring.
- **Warm the top of funnel** at the only channel that converts: Will's personal/founder lane.

The flywheel: every door produces a first event -> single-site-deep is our biggest expander class -> recurring quarterly/annual -> whole-team/remote is the expansion story. Healthcare funds make the recurring spend painless.

## 3. The levers (target / mechanism / proof / status / next)

**A. Core company sales (the engine)**
- Target: HR/People, workplace experience, office managers at talent-competitive employers ~250-5,000 (tech-enabled + elite professional services). HR/People = best close ratio; office manager = biggest volume.
- Mechanism: positioning -> the site -> proposal. Lead with the outcome, publish a price range, arm the champion to sell the CFO.
- Proof: 90%+ booked, 87% rebook, DraftKings/BCG.
- Channel reality: cold converts ~1%; personal ~25x. Cold's only job is to manufacture a warm reply cheaply, then graduate it to the personal lane.
- Next: keep site + proposal on the v4 spine; route warm replies to the personal lane.

**B. Healthcare funds — broker side (channel)**
- Target: tiered benefits brokers (Marsh/Mercer, Aon/NFP, Gallagher, WTW, Lockton; mid-market OneDigital/Sequoia/Newfront/EPIC) + carrier HECs at Cigna/Aetna/Anthem (nearly unprospected).
- Mechanism: white-label wellness for their book + "help clients spend unused carrier wellness dollars before year-end." Tier 1 = "look like a hero" (no rev share); Tier 2/3 = 5-10% rev share.
- Play: aim high, expect the forward. Our one booked broker meeting came from the President of EPIC forwarding down to the person who books.
- Status: crm_target_firms + broker columns + Brokers tab LIVE; 134 contacts.
- Next: principal-level notes only; skip individual-contributor consultants (0 replies from them).

**C. Healthcare funds — client side (the budget unlock)**
- Target: employers whose medical carrier has an employer wellness fund (Cigna Health Improvement Fund, Aetna Wellness Allowance, Anthem Wellness Fund).
- Mechanism: they forward our invoice to the carrier; the carrier pays. Removes the "no budget / out of pocket" objection entirely.
- Proof (the receipt that makes it real): Burberry pays for Shortcut chair massage through their Aetna Wellness Allowance.
- Constraint: only fund-eligible services in THIS pitch — chair massage, assisted stretch, sound baths, mindfulness, nutrition coaching (NOT nails/facials/headshots).
- Status: /wellness-funds client one-pager approved 2026-07-02, build pending.
- Next: ship the one-pager; lead both broker and client conversations with the fund unlock.

**D. Conferences & exhibitors (event channel)**
- One live page (/conference) serves conferences, retreats, offsites. Two distinct buyers:
  1. Companies running their own conference/offsite -> "the touch on the agenda attendees actually show up for."
  2. Companies EXHIBITING at a conference -> the sponsorship/booth play: "10x the conversations, every sign-up a lead." This is our own Workhuman booth model, sold to others.
- Proof: Workhuman Live 2026, 400 massages / 200+ waitlist / 5 chairs.
- Pricing: stations $650-$3,000 "starting at". Bundles drafted but NOT shipped and floors flagged mispriced. Use stations; fix bundles before quoting.
- Next: the exhibitor/booth angle is underused and high-fit for talent-brands at trade shows.

**E. Company retreats / offsites**
- Same page/offer; "we handle the details" for a distributed team's in-person gather. Even fully remote companies gather (kickoffs, offsites, team weeks) = full-menu in-person moments.
- Next: pair with the client fund unlock where the carrier allows event wellness.

**F. CLE — the credibility wedge (attorneys)**
- LIVE: one 60-min accredited CLE, 1.0 credit in Ethics & Professionalism, NY / PA / FL only. Shortcut is the accredited provider and handles submission, attendance, reporting. $3,000 flat, any size. CLE + Wellness Day = custom quote.
- Why it's special: a regulator-recognized deliverable no wellness competitor can grant. In rooms full of skeptics (bar associations, benefits pros), lead with the CLE, not participation stats. A non-wellness door into elite firms -> land-and-expand into full wellness.
- In-market headline: "Your attorneys need ethics credits. Make this the one they thank you for."
- WARNING / expansion flag (NOT built): insurance and financial-professional CE are a DIFFERENT regulatory regime (separate boards + accreditation). Attractive, but today we only have attorney CLE in 3 states. Do NOT claim insurance/financial CE until accreditation is real (the same rule that got CA/TX pulled from the CLE page).
- Next: work NY/PA/FL law firms; scope insurance/financial CE accreditation as a real project before selling it.

**G. (Cross-cutting) The personal/founder lane**
- Will's warm networking (will@ + LinkedIn), brokers first, tech-execs second. The only channel that converts at scale (~25x cold). Every other lever feeds it a reason to reach out.
