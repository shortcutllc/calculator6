# Brain: small brokers and benefits consultants

The job reads this file at the start of every run and rewrites it at the end. It is the only place
the job remembers anything. Keep it under 150 lines: when it grows, merge and delete.

Goal (Will, Nov 9 2026): reach hundreds of small brokerage houses and independent benefits
consultants a week, through as many honest touch points as we can find. Cold email is one channel
with a hard ceiling (spam risk to will@, low reply rate), so it runs daily at a small fixed volume
and the job's real work is finding and proving the other channels.

## How the brain works

1. Numbers come from `broker.mjs score` and the send log. Never estimate a number.
2. A belief needs evidence with a count. "Forms reply better" is not a belief; "forms 3 of 40,
   principal email 1 of 30, as of Nov 20" is.
3. At most 2 experiments run at once. Each changes ONE thing, names the number that would prove it
   wrong, and has an end date. An experiment with no result by its end date is closed as "no signal".
4. The brain proposes, Will decides. A new channel, a new message type or a changed word is a
   proposal with the exact draft attached. Once Will approves, the text is locked in `broker.mjs`
   and the brain never edits it.
5. Every run adds one researched channel idea or advances one already in the backlog. Research
   means opening real pages and counting how many of the cohort the channel reaches.
6. Every Friday: read all numbers, kill what lost, scale what won, and write the week in 5 lines
   under Weekly review. Kill the brain's own rules too when the numbers say they are wrong.
